How it works

One platform that checks every claim against the therapist’s session notes, the way an insurer would.

Before billing

Caught before it goes out

Each new claim is checked against its session note before it’s sent.

Every month

Paid claims, rechecked

Claims already paid are rechecked automatically, with dollars at risk by therapist and an early warning on unusual billing.

When the letter arrives

A records request, handled

On demand: a 48-hour review of the requested charts and a response packet.

People in the loop

Every fix is approved by a person

Each flag goes to a behavioral-health biller before the owner sees it. Clinicians decide what to change. We never write or edit clinical notes.

Underneath

Each insurer’s rules, versioned

A library of each insurer’s rules, dated and versioned, so every flag cites its source. Works with whatever records software the practice already uses.

Pricing

Simple, per practice

$99 a month for the whole platform. A records-request review is $490 for up to 40 charts, and triage is free.

Privacy

Mental-health records are as private as data gets. Privacy comes first.

01

No real records first

Nothing real reaches us before the practice signs a privacy agreement (a HIPAA business associate agreement).

02

Never psychotherapy notes

Only the progress notes insurers can request. Therapists’ private notes stay with them.

03

Names removed

Client names come off before anything is checked.

04

Deleted after each review

Every file is erased when the review ends.
